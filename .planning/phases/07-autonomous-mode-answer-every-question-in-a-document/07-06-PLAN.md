<objective>
Prove the phase, honestly, and tell the truth about the new permission.

Two things stand between "built" and "done". First, the operator's own framing has to
work end to end: *a project leader sends a worker a document full of questions; the
worker opens it, picks fast or slow, and the answers get written in.* Second, the
extension now asks for access to a person's Google documents — that has to be described
in the privacy policy and the store listing before anyone is asked to grant it, not after.

Purpose: on this machine "code green" has never meant "feature works". The automated
suite covers the two editors a browser can test without a Google login; the third needs
the operator's eyes, and this plan is where that happens and gets written down.
Output: the acceptance scenario as a test, truthful permission disclosure, and a recorded
walkthrough against all five ROADMAP success criteria.
</objective>
